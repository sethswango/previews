A Rising Change: copy brief

Page-by-page SEO notes for the copywriting · from Seth · August 2026

Where I'm coming from

1. The existing writing is the site's biggest strength. Lines like “overwhelmed, overworked, overlooked. over it.” stand out in this field. Everything below is about adding search specificity around the voice expressed, not rewriting it. The copy side is yours; if anything here fights the writing, the writing wins and I'll find another way from the SEO side.

2. The practice is statewide, not St. Louis. Telehealth only, licensed across Missouri, and most of the state has almost no affirming providers. Work “anywhere in Missouri” naturally into every page.

3. The unclaimed position is queer-affirming perinatal care in Missouri. Verified: the closest competitor's perinatal page speaks only to moms and women. Savannah has the PSI training, the affirming practice, and the statewide license. From the search side, this is the one I'd weight heaviest.

The SEO checklist, applies to every page

  • Set the main heading block to Heading 1 in Squarespace (7 of 8 pages currently have none).
  • One H1 per page, containing the page's main phrase. Never split a heading across two text blocks.
  • Main phrase appears in the first 100 words, naturally. If a sentence reads awkwardly, it is not helping.
  • Meta descriptions at 150 to 160 characters, ending with the free 15-minute consultation.
  • Add alt text to images (one per page is missing it).

The pages

1. Homepage (about 2 hours, do first)

Title tag
Online Therapy for Women and Queer Adults in Missouri | A Rising Change
H1
Online Therapy for Women and Queer Adults, Anywhere in Missouri. Keep “care. connection. change.” directly beneath as the tagline; it does emotional work, it just can't carry the heading slot.
Body
Grow to 700 to 900 words: who she works with, fully virtual and statewide (say it early), the out-of-network model stated plainly, her intersectional feminist framework, free consultation as the single next step.

2. Perinatal page (about 1.5 hours, highest strategic value)

Title tag
Perinatal and Postpartum Therapy in Missouri | PMAD Specialist | A Rising Change
H1
Perinatal and Postpartum Therapy, Available Anywhere in Missouri
Meta description (currently empty, must be written)
Perinatal mental health therapy for pregnancy and postpartum anxiety, depression, and PMADs, available virtually anywhere in Missouri. Savannah Helton, LCSW, trained through Postpartum Support International. Free 15-minute consultation.
The differentiating move
Explicitly welcome queer parents, non-birthing parents, and partners, alongside moms rather than instead of them. Use “birthing people” and “parents” in the mix. No Missouri competitor does this.
Credentials
Work in her Postpartum Support International training and seven years with high-risk pregnancies. Grow toward 1,000 to 1,200 words.

5. New page: out-of-network and fees, plainly explained (about 1.5 hours)

Targets
out of network therapist missouri · superbill therapy missouri · therapist who doesn't take insurance
Content
Why out-of-network (privacy, no diagnosis required for billing, no session caps), how superbills work step by step, a literal script for calling your insurer about out-of-network benefits, FSA/HSA, the tiered equity fee.
Context
St. Louis average is around $200 a session; she charges $125 to $135. From a positioning angle, accessibility lands better than discount, but the telling is yours.

6. New page: therapy anywhere in Missouri (about 1.5 hours)

Targets
online therapist missouri · virtual therapy missouri
Content
One honest statewide page: that she serves all of Missouri, naming St. Louis, Kansas City, Springfield, Columbia, Jefferson City, Cape Girardeau, Joplin, and rural Missouri naturally in prose; what a video session is like; privacy; what you need to attend.
Caution
One real page, not a batch of near-identical city pages. Templated city pages are a known spam pattern and would put rankings at risk.

7. Quick passes (about 1 hour total)

FAQ
Add H1 “Frequently Asked Questions About Therapy.” Keep every question as its own subheading so FAQ markup can be added afterward. Consider adding: Do you take insurance? Can I see you if I don't live in St. Louis? What happens in a free consultation?
Meet Savannah
Title: Savannah Helton, LCSW | Queer-Affirming Therapist in Missouri. Add H1. Make sure LCSW, license 2019031582, and the PSI training appear as text, not only in an image.

Questions to route to Savannah through Ashley

  1. Years of experience: site and directories currently show 13, 15+, and 20. Which is right? One number everywhere.
  2. Is the Psychology Today phone number (314-370-2631) an intentional tracking line, or should it match the site?
  3. Queer page scope: women, or folks more broadly?
  4. How prominently does she want the queer + perinatal specialty stated? Strongest recommendation in the report, and entirely her call.

If the hours run short (my read from the SEO side)

Drop in this order: Meet Savannah expansion (do title and H1 only), FAQ additions (still do its H1), therapy-for-women expansion. The ones I'd keep no matter what: the H1 fixes, the homepage title, and the perinatal meta description. Minutes of work, highest search return in the whole plan.
